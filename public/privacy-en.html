CardTalk privacy policy

November 21, 2026

Conversations and bookmarks

No account is needed. Players speak to one another. CardTalk does not record or collect their answers. Bookmarks, language, seats, rounds and the current card order are stored on this device. CardTalk does not send them to a developer server. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local records. Offloading the app keeps them. Remove a bookmark in the app or clear app storage in Android settings to erase local records. Your operating system’s backup settings may apply.

Android advertising

Only the Android version shows ads. A Google AdMob banner may appear after a game. Google’s advertising and consent tools may process or share IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, advertising and app set IDs, interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing depends on your region, settings and choices. CardTalk does not include answers or bookmarks in ad requests.

Where applicable, Google presents privacy choices before the app requests ads. Ads are requested only when Google’s consent tool allows them. Where available, reopen Ad privacy choices in How to play. Cards work offline.

Sharing and contact

Sharing is available only on Android. Share CardTalk opens the Android share sheet with a sample question. You choose the recipient and app. For privacy questions, contact the developer, HYUNJUN YOOK, at contact@dusondream.com.
