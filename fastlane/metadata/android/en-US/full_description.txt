“What caught your eye on the way here?”

When introductions are over, pick a deck and let a question start the conversation. Two to six people share one phone. Choose one or two rounds, flip a card, then pass clockwise. Swap a question or pass without answering.

First hellos starts with everyday encounters. Favorite things explores tastes and little enthusiasms. Everyday life looks closer at familiar routines. A little closer makes room for more personal stories.

Each deck has 20 questions, each with its own optional follow-up. Bookmark a favorite or resume a game later. Play in English or Korean and switch languages without losing your place.

Play offline after installation. No sign-in or written answers are required. On Android, a banner ad may appear when a game ends.
